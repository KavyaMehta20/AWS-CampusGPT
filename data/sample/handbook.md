# Sample University Student Handbook (SAMPLE DATA ONLY)

## Section 1: Academic Advising
At Sample University, academic advising is mandatory before registering for courses each semester. Students must meet with their academic advisor to review their degree audits and remove registration holds.
Advising appointments are scheduled through the Portal system.

## Section 2: Course Registration Policies
Undergraduate students may register for up to 18 credit hours per semester without special permission.
Taking more than 18 credit hours requires an approved Course Overload Request signed by the Department Chair.
The add/drop deadline is Friday of the second week of classes at 11:59 PM.

## Section 3: Graduation Requirements
To qualify for a Bachelor of Science degree at Sample University:
1. Complete a minimum of 120 credit hours.
2. Maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0.
3. Complete the university writing requirement (WRIT 101) with a grade of C or higher.
4. Fulfill all core curriculum and major elective requirements.

## Section 4: Academic Integrity Code
Sample University holds students to high ethical standards. Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, and cheating on examinations will result in referral to the Academic Conduct Board. Penalties range from course failure to academic suspension.
